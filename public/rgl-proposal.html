Section 01Executive summary

执行摘要

Five thousand and seventy-six people have contacted this business since December. Thirty-five signed. Of the rest, 2,829 have no salesperson, no outcome and no reason recorded against them, so nobody can say whether we turned them down or simply lost them.

自去年 12 月以来，共有 5,076 人主动联系过这家公司，其中 35 人签约。在其余的人当中，有 2,829 条既没有对接销售、没有结果，也没有任何原因记录——因此没有人说得清，这些人是我们拒绝的，还是就这么丢了。

This proposal sets out how to build the RGL ecosystem — website and web application, booking, checkout and payment, and four portals covering staff, clients, customer relationships and content. It starts with that gap, because closing it costs ten weeks and buys back the only asset the business is currently throwing away.

本提案说明如何建设红桥全球旅居生态平台——官网与网页应用、预订、结算与支付，以及员工、客户、客户关系与内容四个门户。它从上述缺口入手：补上这个缺口需要十周，换回的是这家公司目前唯一在白白丢弃的资产。
Recommendation · 建议

Approve Phase 1 — ten weeks, existing internal capacity, no blocking decisions, no new subscriptions — and answer the four decisions in Section 15 that block Phase 2. Section 11.0 sets out the alternatives and why they are weaker.

批准第一阶段——十周、由现有内部人力承担、无前置决策阻碍、无新增订阅支出——并就第 15 节中阻碍第二阶段的四项决策给出答复。第 11.0 节列出了其他备选路径，以及它们为何更弱。

This is not a proposal to build less than the Product Book sets out. We will build all of it. We will start with the parts the evidence says pay back first, because working down the list in the order it happens to be written in is how the money ends up in the wrong place.

本提案并非建议缩减《产品册》的范围，而是全部建设，但建设次序由证据决定，而非由组件被列出的先后顺序决定。

The proposal in six points

提案要点六条

1. One platform, eight surfaces, two systems of record. The website, the web app, booking, checkout and the four portals should all be windows onto the same underlying system. One record of who the client is. One record of what they owe and have paid. Build each piece as its own product and they stop agreeing with each other. The client who paid in the checkout is a different client in the CRM, and reconciling the two becomes somebody's monthly job. That is the most common and most expensive way projects of this shape go wrong.

一、一个平台、八个界面、两套记录中枢。官网、网页应用、预订、结算与四个门户，都是同一中枢之上的界面：一套客户主记录，一套资金记录。把它们当作彼此独立的产品来建设，是此类项目中最常见、也最昂贵的失败方式。

2. Start where the money is, not at the top of the list. The company's own lead data points to how leads are handled after they arrive. Of 5,076 leads in the register, 38.7% have a sales rep named against them and the register records 8 closes against the 35 the business reports. The unnamed majority were not all neglected. Sales screens out enquirers who don't fit, and that's a legitimate commercial judgement. But only 271 leads carry a refusal reason of any kind. A further 2,829, or 55.7% of the register, have no rep, no outcome and no reason recorded at all. So the business cannot tell a lead it correctly refused from one it simply dropped, and neither can anyone reading its numbers. Closing that gap is Phase 1, and it does not depend on a decision from anyone.

二、按回报排序，而非按清单顺序。公司自身的线索数据指向线索进来之后的处理环节。台账共 5,076 条线索，其中 38.7% 填有对接销售；台账记录成交 8 笔，而业务方口径为 35 笔。未填销售的大多数并非都被忽略：销售会筛掉不符合客户画像的咨询，这属于正当的商业判断。但带有任何拒绝原因的线索仅 271 条。另有 2,829 条——占台账 55.7%——既无对接销售、无结果，也无任何原因记录。因此公司无法区分已正确拒绝与单纯流失，任何看这份数据的人也无法区分。补上这个缺口就是第一阶段的内容，且不依赖任何人作出决策。

3. Then the money layer. Milestone billing for a A$220,000–270,000 engagement, in AUD and CNY, across bank transfer, PayID, WeChat Pay and Alipay, with the payment rails assigned by product tier rather than offered uniformly, because the wallets cannot carry six-figure stages.

三、其次是资金层。面向 22–27 万澳元委托的里程碑分期收费，支持澳元与人民币，通过银行转账、PayID、微信支付与支付宝收款——支付通道按产品层级分配，而非一律全开，因为电子钱包无法承载六位数金额的分期款。

4. Then commerce, portals and content. Booking, checkout, the client portal and the CMS follow, serving the products that the first two phases prove there is demand for.

四、再建设交易、门户与内容。预订、结算、客户门户与内容管理系统随后建设，服务于前两阶段已验证有需求的产品。

5. Compliance has to be built into the system, not written into a policy. The eight boundaries set out in the Product Book each become a working control: an approval step before marketing claims are published, disclosure of related-party relationships, payee types the system will not accept, milestones described by the work delivered, and evidence recorded as that work is done. Including it now is cheap. Adding it afterwards, and being believed, is not.

五、合规是建出来的，不是写出来的。《产品册》第 08 节的八项边界，逐项转化为具体的系统控制——营销主张审核关卡、关联方披露、禁止的收款方类型、以已交付工作定义的里程碑，以及在工作发生时同步留存的证据。现在做成本很低；事后再补，其可信度无法成立。

6. Ten of these screens already run. The IT function built a staff and client portal for RedBridge Consulting and offered it for testing in March 2026. Pipeline, case milestones, document requests, the client portal and staff messaging are working today. Phase 1 is therefore ten weeks of work on a system that already runs. Routing, deduplication, response timing and reporting, plus the single change that matters most: connecting the portal to where leads actually arrive. About 1.3% of leads reach the website form. The rest arrive on Xiaohongshu. That gap, not the software, is the likeliest reason nobody tried the portal, and Section 8.5 addresses it.

六、其中相当一部分已经建成。已为红桥咨询建成一套员工与客户门户，并于 2026 年 3 月开放测试。销售管道、案件里程碑、材料索取、客户门户与员工内部沟通目前均可运行。因此第一阶段是十周的工作量，而非从零开始的建设——自动分配、去重、响应计时与报表——外加最关键的一项改动：把门户接到线索真正进来的地方。仅约 1.3% 的线索经由官网表单进入，其余来自小红书。这一断口而非软件本身，才是当初无人试用该门户的最可能原因，第 8.5 节专门处理它。

1.1 The triage inbox

1.1 分诊收件箱

Sales already declines leads that don't fit a A$220,000 engagement, and that judgement is sound. What the register shows is that the judgement is almost never written down. 271 leads carry a refusal reason. 2,829 carry nothing at all, so a lead that was correctly refused and a lead that was simply dropped look identical on the record.

销售本来就会拒掉不适合 22 万澳元委托的线索，这个判断是合理的。台账反映出的问题是：这个判断几乎从不被写下来。带有拒绝原因的线索有 271 条，完全没有任何记录的有 2,829 条——因此被正确拒绝的线索与单纯被漏掉的线索在记录上完全无法区分。

The fix is a triage inbox rather than an assignment queue. Imported leads land there scored, and management works the inbox instead of the spreadsheet. Two buttons: admit, which routes the lead by rule and starts the response clock, or decline, which asks for a reason from a short list and archives it. Declining takes one click and one reason. That single field turns 2,829 unexplained rows into a record the business can audit, count and learn from.

解决办法是分诊收件箱，而不是分配队列。导入的线索带着评分进入收件箱，管理层处理收件箱，而不再处理表格。只有两个动作：准入——按规则自动分配并启动响应计时；拒绝——从简短的选项中选一个原因，随后归档。拒绝只需一次点击加一个原因。正是这一个字段，把 2,829 条无法解释的记录，变成可审计、可统计、可从中学习的数据。
Declined is not deleted · 拒绝不等于删除

Declined leads stay visible to management with their reason and the name of whoever declined them, and they stay reportable. Silent archiving would reproduce the exact gap this section exists to close, and in a licensed business an unexplained refusal is a record worth keeping.

被拒绝的线索对管理层仍然可见，并保留拒绝原因与操作人姓名，也仍然可以纳入报表。静默归档只会重新制造本节要消除的那个缺口；而在持牌业务中，一条没有说明理由的拒绝记录，是你应当留存的证据，不是应当丢弃的负担。

1.2 Scoring, and what it can honestly do

1.2 评分机制及其现实边界

Each lead arrives with a score built from what the register already captures well: whether the person is onshore (about 80% are), their visa type and expiry, years of experience and sector, and English test results where stated. The score orders the inbox. It does not decide anything.

每条线索进入时带有一个评分，其依据是台账目前已经采集得比较完整的字段：是否已在澳洲境内（约 80% 是）、签证类型与到期时间、工作年限与所属行业，以及已填写的英语成绩。评分只决定收件箱的排序，不决定任何结果。

That limit is deliberate and worth stating plainly to anyone who asks. The register records 8 closes against the 35 the business reports, so there are not enough recorded outcomes to fit weights to. Any weighting proposed today is judgement expressed as a number. Presenting it as calibration would not survive the first question asked of it.

这个限制是有意设定的，且值得对任何提问者直说。台账记录成交 8 笔，而业务方口径为 35 笔——已记录的结果数量根本不足以拟合权重。今天提出的任何权重组合，都只是把经验判断写成数字。若将其表述为基于历史数据校准，经不起第一个追问。

So the weights start as a written-down version of how the team already decides, they are visible to management, and they are adjusted quarterly once admitted leads have produced enough outcomes to check them against. Recording the decline reasons is what generates that evidence. The scoring model comes out of Phase 1's data discipline. It cannot go in ahead of it.

因此，权重的初始版本就是把团队现有的判断标准写下来，对管理层完全可见，并在准入线索积累出足够结果之后按季度校正。而记录拒绝原因，正是产生这些证据的来源。评分模型是第一阶段数据纪律的产出，而不是它的前提。

Hard limits are separate from the score. A small number of conditions send a lead straight to triage flagged for review, with the reason shown, rather than being auto-declined. Anything touching visa history or eligibility is reviewed by the MARA Agent at Insight Idea. No rule resolves it, because that judgement sits with the licence.

硬性限制与评分相互独立。少数几类情形会让线索直接进入分诊并标记需复核，同时显示触发原因，而不是被自动拒绝。凡涉及签证历史或资格判断的情形，一律由 Insight Idea 的 MARA 持牌代理复核，不由规则自行决定——因为这项判断属于牌照的责任范围。

Sales screening out an unsuitable enquirer is a commercial judgement, and a reasonable one. But it accounts for 271 leads. The 2,829 with nothing recorded at all are not screened, refused or lost. They are unresolved on paper, so nobody can say which is which. The gate isn't assignment. It's the disposition that never gets recorded, and it sits before assignment, not at it.

销售筛掉不合适的咨询是一种商业判断，且是合理的判断。但这类线索共 271 条。而那 2,829 条没有任何记录的线索，既不算被筛选、被拒绝，也不算流失——它们只是在纸面上没有结论，因此没有人能分清它们属于哪一类。闸门不是分配，而是那个从未被记录下来的处置结论；而且它发生在分配之前，不是在分配这一步。

3.1a 线索实际从哪里进来

The website form has produced 64 enquiries between 2 May and 14 August 2026, plus 11 on the earlier site version from 18 March. Monthly: 35 in May, 8 in June, 17 in July, 4 to mid-August. Set against the inbound register, this is the single most important structural fact in the document. The website accounts for roughly 1.3% of total lead flow; something close to 98.7% arrives through Xiaohongshu and does not pass through the website form at all.

The existing portal was designed on the assumption that leads enter via the website. That assumption is correct in design and wrong in fact, and it is the likeliest explanation for why nobody tried the portal. A sales team will not open a system that contains one lead in eighty. Section 8.5 addresses it as a plumbing problem, because that is what it is.

现有门户的设计前提是线索经由官网进入。这个前提在设计上没有错，但与事实不符——而这很可能就是无人试用该门户的真正原因：一个八十条线索里只装了一条的系统，销售团队不会用它。这是管道接通问题，不是意愿问题；第 8.5 节专门处理它。

3.2 What this implies

3.2 由此得出的推论

The Product Book's underlying instincts hold up well. Own the client relationship instead of brokering it. Build a data asset that gains value over time. Serve a whole lifecycle, not one transaction. Never promise a government outcome. What the data changes is where the emphasis should sit. The people contacting the business today have already decided to migrate and are already living in Australia. So the platform's first job is to respond to that demand faster and more reliably than it does now. Selling additional things to those people comes after that, not before it.

《产品册》的战略直觉是成立的：自持客户关系而非居间转介、建设可复利累积的数据资产、服务生命周期而非单次交易、不承诺政府审批结果。数据补充的是侧重点的修正：今天的需求在进线时已经具备明确意向，且人已在澳洲境内。因此平台的首要任务是更快、更可靠地响应既有需求——之后才谈得上向其销售更多产品。

Section 04Design principles

设计原则

Seven principles sit behind every decision in the rest of this document. If a later section seems to pick one option over another without explaining itself, the reason is usually here.

以下七条原则支配后文的每一项决定。若后续章节中某个选择看似武断，那是因为它由其中某一条原则所决定。

One client, one record

一个客户，一份档案

Someone who buys a A$120 experience and someone who signs a quarter-million-dollar engagement are the same kind of record in the system. Everything else (the booking, the case, the invoice, the family member who paid) hangs off that one record.

购买 120 澳元体验活动的人，与签下 25 万澳元委托的人，在系统中属于同一类记录。其余一切都是与该记录之间的关系。

Configuration over code

配置优先于代码

Products, prices, milestone schedules, fee stages and visa pathways should all be changeable without a developer. Australian visa rules move, prices vary from one client to the next, and the Group will cut the product list back at some point. If any of those changes needs code, the platform will feel out of date within a year.

产品、价格、里程碑安排、收费阶段与路径定义都是数据，不是代码。澳洲签证政策会变；产品定价因客户而异；集团也会在某个时点裁减产品线。若上述任一项的调整都需要开发人员介入，这个平台在一年之内就会严重老化。

Compliance as a mechanism, not a memo

合规是机制，不是通知

Each boundary in Section 08 of the Product Book becomes a control with someone accountable for it, a record of what happened, and the ability to stop an action that breaches it. A rule that only works when the person typing remembers it isn't a control.

《产品册》第 08 节的每一条边界，都要落成系统中的一项控制，具备责任人、审计留痕与拦截行为。凡是依赖录入者记得的规则，都不算控制。

Evidence captured as a by-product of work

证据随工作自然生成

Deliverables, approvals, disclosures and consents get recorded at the moment they happen, not pieced together afterwards. In three years, that is the difference between a record that holds and one that doesn't.

里程碑交付物、审批、披露与同意，均在发生当时记录，而非事后追补。这正是多年之后这份记录仍然可信的原因。

WeChat-first, not email-first

以微信为先，而非以邮件为先

Almost all the contact details in the register are WeChat; very few are email addresses. While Chinese-market clients remain the core revenue, notifications, follow-up and client identity all work on that basis, with email as the backup. If the Group takes the products global, email becomes the primary channel and the platform has to carry both without a rebuild.

台账中的联系方式绝大多数是微信，邮箱覆盖率很低。只要中国市场客户仍是核心收入来源，通知、跟进与身份识别就以微信为主通道、邮件为备用。若集团将产品推向全球市场，邮件将成为主通道——平台必须同时支持两者，且无需重建。

Buy the commodity, build the differentiator

通用能力外采，差异化能力自建

Payment processing, document storage, e-signature, messaging and analytics are all things to buy. The lead engine, the case model, the milestone billing logic and the employer network are things to build, because they are what the business actually is.

支付处理、文件存储、电子签名、邮件与分析工具，一律外采。线索引擎、案件模型、里程碑收费逻辑与雇主网络，一律自建——因为它们就是业务本身。

Nothing ships without measurement

没有度量，不予上线

Before we build a phase, we agree which numbers it should move. The platform then reports them. A business whose own register understated its signed clients by a factor of four cannot afford to build something else it has no way of measuring.

每个阶段在动工之前先明确它要改变哪些数字，并由平台负责呈现这些数字。一家连自己成交了多少单都统计不准的公司，无法管理一个自己无法度量的平台。

5.4 E-signature: build or buy

5.4 电子签名：自建还是外采

Can it be built? Yes. The mechanics are not exotic. Capture a drawn or typed signature, hash the document, record signer identity, email verification, IP address, user agent and timestamp, then seal a PDF with an audit trail appended. This can be built in two to three weeks.

能自建吗？能。技术上并不复杂：采集手写或输入的签名、对文件做哈希、记录签署人身份、邮箱验证、IP、浏览器信息与时间戳，最后生成附带审计留痕的密封 PDF。该功能约需两到三周完成。

Is it legally effective? Under Australian electronic transactions legislation, an electronic signature is generally valid where the signer is identified, their intention is indicated, the method is as reliable as appropriate for the purpose, and the other party consents. A self-built system can satisfy that. So the question is not legality.

法律上有效吗？依据澳洲电子交易相关立法，电子签名在满足以下条件时通常有效：能识别签署人、能体现其签署意图、所用方法就用途而言足够可靠、且对方同意采用。自建系统可以满足这些条件。因此问题不在于合法性。

The question is evidentiary. If a A$220,000–270,000 engagement is ever disputed, the signature has to survive challenge, and the firm would be defending its own implementation instead of pointing at an industry-standard certificate and a vendor who produces a court-ready audit trail. Add key management, tamper-evidence, long-term validation and ongoing maintenance, and the cost of a build exceeds a subscription many times over.

真正的问题在于举证。一旦 22–27 万澳元的委托发生争议，签名必须经得起质疑——而那时你要为自己的实现方式辩护，而不是出示一份行业标准证书、并由服务商提供可直接呈堂的审计记录。再加上密钥管理、防篡改、长期有效性验证与持续维护，自建的总成本远高于订阅费用。

For the Client Agreement, the signature stays on paper. Of the eight signings the register records, six have a documented in-person meeting and seven are onshore, six of those in Melbourne. The firm also has a Chengdu branch, so face-to-face signing is available on both sides of the relationship. At roughly 35 signings a year, a signature given in person, scanned and filed against the record, is the strongest evidence available and costs nothing. Thirty-five sheets of paper a year is a cheap way to remove the argument entirely from the one document carrying a quarter of a million dollars.

《客户协议》的签署保留纸质形式。台账记录的 8 笔成交中，6 笔有明确的线下面谈记录，7 笔客户在澳洲境内（其中 6 笔在墨尔本）。公司在成都亦设有分支，因此当面签署在中澳两端都具备条件。按每年约 35 份签约计算，当面签署、扫描并归档的签名，是可获得的最强证据，且成本为零。每年 35 页纸，就能把争议从这份涉及 25 万澳元的文件上彻底移除，这个代价很便宜。

Everything else signs electronically in the portal: engagement letters, variations, consents, acknowledgements, and any internal document where an audit trail is useful but a dispute is not plausible.

其余文件在门户内电子签署：委托说明函、变更协议、各类同意书与确认函，以及任何留痕有用、但几乎不可能产生争议的内部文件。
What hardening fixes, and what it doesn't · 加固能解决与不能解决的问题

Three measures raise the built system's evidentiary strength at no cost. An independent timestamp on every executed document, issued by a public authority outside the company. A copy emailed to the signer as they sign, so they hold evidence through a channel neither party controls. And storage the company itself cannot alter afterwards. Together these answer most of what a vendor's certificate answers.

有三项措施可以零成本提升自建系统的举证强度：由公司之外的公共权威机构，对每份已签署文件签发独立时间戳；在客户签署的同时把副本发送至其邮箱，使其通过双方都不控制的渠道持有证据；以及采用公司自身事后也无法更改的存储方式。三者合计，可以覆盖服务商证书所能回答问题的大部分。

What they do not remove is that the system belongs to us, and that we administer it. On a document worth A$220,000–270,000 that residual is not worth carrying when paper is free.

它们无法消除的是：这套系统归我方所有、由我方管理。对于一份价值 22–27 万澳元的文件而言，在纸质签署完全免费的前提下，这部分残余风险不值得承担。

The Client Agreement should include a clause in which the client consents to electronic execution and accepts the portal's audit record as evidence. The legislation already requires consent. Obtaining it in writing, in advance, closes a line of argument before it opens.

《客户协议》中应加入条款，由客户确认同意以电子方式签署，并接受门户的留痕记录作为证据。相关立法本就要求取得同意；事先以书面形式取得，可以在争议出现之前就关闭这条抗辩路径。
Send on a click, not on a stage change · 由点击触发，而非由阶段变更触发

Because the firm agrees price per engagement across a range, a representative generates and sends each contract deliberately, working from a preview. The system never fires one when someone changes a dropdown. Automatic dispatch on stage change would eventually put a wrongly-priced contract in front of a client, and the firm cannot recover from that.

价格由公司按每份委托在区间内单独议定，因此由销售在预览后主动生成并发出合同；系统绝不会因某人改动下拉选项而自动触发。若以阶段变更自动发送，迟早会让客户拿到一份价格错误的合同，而公司无法挽回这类错误。

7.3 Milestone billing

7.3 里程碑收费

The flagship engagement is A$220,000–270,000 excluding legal fees, billed across four stages. Because the total varies per client and the stage split is set per contract, the system stores the milestone schedule against each engagement and bills from the signed contract rather than from a global price list. This is why the stage split being unconfirmed does not block the build.

旗舰委托为 22–27 万澳元（不含法律费用），分四个阶段收取。由于总额因客户而异、各阶段拆分由合同逐份约定，因此里程碑安排作为数据挂在每份委托之下，系统依据已签合同出账，而非依据统一价目表。这也正是阶段拆分尚未确定不会阻碍建设的原因。

Three rules govern the design:

设计遵循三条规则：
  • Milestones are defined by work delivered. An event occurring does not release a payment. The invoice line, the contract schedule and the system's milestone definition all use the same wording. 里程碑以已交付的工作定义，而非以某事件的发生定义。发票行、合同附表与系统中的里程碑定义，三者使用完全相同的措辞。
  • The system records deliverable evidence against each stage as the work happens — searches run, employers approached, screening completed, documents prepared. 各阶段的交付证据在工作发生时同步留存——已开展的搜寻、已接洽的雇主、已完成的筛选、已准备的材料。
  • Legal fees never pass through the platform. They are invoiced by the firm under its own arrangements and are excluded from platform revenue entirely. 法律费用绝不经由本平台。由律所按其自身安排开票，完全排除在平台收入之外。

7.4 Payers

7.4 付款人

Payment by someone other than the client is existing practice and, at these values, will be common. The payer is therefore a first-class record, carrying identity, relationship to the client, and the authority under which they paid. Not a note in a comment field. Three consequences follow:

由客户本人以外的人付款是既有做法，且在这一金额区间会相当普遍。因此付款人必须是一类独立的正式记录——包含身份、与客户的关系，以及付款所依据的授权——而不是备注栏里的一句话。由此产生三点后果：
  • Refunds return to the original payer, not to the client. 退款退回原付款人，而非退给客户。
  • The invoice and the payment may name different parties; who the tax invoice addresses is an accounting decision we need before building receipting. 发票抬头与实际付款人可能不是同一方；税务发票开给谁，是在建设收据功能之前必须由会计给出的决定。
  • An employer can never be a permitted payer of a client fee. The ledger blocks that payer type outright, rather than leaving it to judgement at data entry. 雇主在任何情况下都不得成为客户费用的付款人。这一点在账务系统中以禁止的付款人类型强制实现，而不是交由录入人员临场判断。

7.5 Quote and approval

7.5 报价与审批

Because price is agreed per engagement across a range, a quote step comes before the contract. Someone prices it, someone approves anything outside an agreed band, and the client accepts a specific schedule. The system records whatever moves the price within the range as a field against every quote, permission-restricted if commercially sensitive, so finance can analyse margin later.

由于价格在一个区间内逐份委托议定，合同之前须有报价环节：由人定价，超出约定区间的由人审批，客户接受一份具体的收费安排。系统会把影响价格在区间内落点的每项因素，作为字段记录在每份报价上（涉及商业敏感的设置权限限制），以便财务日后分析毛利。

8.2 The lead engine

8.2 线索引擎

This is Phase 1 and the highest-value component in the proposal.

这是第一阶段，也是本提案中价值最高的组件。

What a first-response SLA is

什么是首次响应时限

An internal commitment to how quickly a new lead receives its first human reply, enforced by the system instead of by good intentions. A clock starts when the lead is created and stops when the first outbound contact is logged. Thresholds trigger escalation automatically. At the first, the assigned representative is notified; at the second, their team leader; at the third, the lead returns to the pool for reassignment. Nobody has to notice.

这是一项关于新线索多久能得到第一次人工回复的内部承诺，由系统强制执行，而不是靠自觉。线索创建时开始计时，记录到第一次外呼／外联时停止。超过设定阈值即自动升级：第一档通知负责顾问，第二档通知其组长，第三档将线索退回公海重新分配。全过程无需任何人注意到。

It matters because in high-intent inbound sales, speed to first contact is the largest controllable influence on conversion, and right now nobody measures it. Two practical notes: measure the median and the 90th percentile, because averages hide the leads that were left for two days; and make the clock business-hours aware across both Australian and Chinese time zones, or every overnight lead will read as a breach.

之所以重要，是因为在高意向的进线销售中，首次接触速度是转化率中最大的一个可控变量——而目前这个指标根本没有被度量。两点实务提醒：既要看中位数，也要看 90 分位数，因为平均值会掩盖那些被搁置两天的线索；此外计时须识别澳洲与中国两地的工作时间，否则每一条夜间线索都会被判为超时。

8.3 The engagement file

8.3 委托档案

Client documents live in Google Docs today, as Insight Idea has always kept them. Passports, visa histories, English test results, testamurs and employment references sit in shared folders. It works, and it has worked for years.

客户材料目前存放在 Google 文档中，这是 Insight Idea 一直以来的做法。护照、签证历史、英语成绩、学历证明与工作证明保存在共享文件夹里。这套做法一直在运转，也已经运转多年。

What it does not do is form a file. A folder holds documents. It does not record which matter they belong to, when the firm asked for them, when they arrived, who opened them afterwards, or when they should be destroyed. Sharing links outlive the engagement that created them. For a A$274,000 matter handled under a MARA Agent's registration, that gap is the exposure worth naming.

但它构不成一份档案。文件夹只是存放材料，并不记录这些材料属于哪个委托、律所何时索取、何时收到、此后由谁调阅，以及何时应当销毁。共享链接的存续时间往往超过产生它的那份委托。对于一份由 MARA 持牌代理经手、金额 27.4 万澳元的案件而言，这才是值得指出的风险敞口。

The portal already produces the missing half. Document requests record what was asked for and when. Uploads record arrival. The case timeline records the stage each document supports. Row-level policies in the database record who may open what, and every access is logged. None of that requires new work. It requires the engagement to be run there.

门户已经具备缺失的那一半能力：材料索取记录要了什么、什么时候要的，上传记录什么时候收到，案件时间线记录该材料支撑哪个节点，数据库的行级策略记录谁可以打开什么，且每一次访问都留痕。这些都不需要新开发，只需要委托在这里运行。
Start with the index, not the files · 先做索引，不搬文件

The portal holds the record of which documents exist, when they were requested, when they arrived and who accessed them. The files themselves can stay where they are for now. Insight Idea keeps working the way it works, and the case file becomes auditable from the first matter rather than the first migration.

门户先承载有哪些材料、何时索取、何时收到、谁调阅过的记录，文件本身暂时留在原处。Insight Idea 的工作方式不变，而案件档案从第一份委托起就具备可审计性，无需等待一次数据迁移。

The legal split then falls out of this rather than being imposed on it. Every case carries a tag: general CRM record, or legal matter. Legal matters open in full only to the legal role. CRM staff see that the matter exists and what stage it has reached, and nothing else. Access is logged in both directions, and the tag is a column in the database rather than a hidden menu, so the rule holds whoever is looking.

法律案件的隔离由此自然生成，而不是外加的一层限制。每个案件标记为一般 CRM 记录或法律案件；法律案件的完整内容仅法务角色可见，CRM 人员只能看到该案件存在及其所处阶段；两个方向的访问均留痕。该标记是数据库中的一个字段，而非界面上的一个隐藏菜单，因此无论由谁查看，规则都成立。

The rule covers records that already exist, not only new ones, so the historical CRM data needs a classification pass before we switch it on. Two things give that pass an end date. Anything ambiguous defaults to legal matter until someone with the legal role reclassifies it, so the safe state is the default state. And one named person from Insight Idea signs off the classified set, because a data-cleaning job with no end date does not get one.

该规则同时覆盖既有记录，而非仅适用于新记录，因此在启用之前，历史 CRM 数据需要先完成一次分类梳理。有两点可以让这次梳理有明确终点：凡归属存疑的一律默认标记为法律案件，直至具备法务角色的人重新分类——安全状态即默认状态；以及由 Insight Idea 指定一人对分类结果签字确认，因为没有截止日期的数据整理工作不会自行产生截止日期。

Retention

留存期限

A file that is never destroyed is as much a liability as one that is destroyed too early. The platform closes a matter on the visa grant or the point the case is lost, holds the documents for the period the Code of Conduct requires from that date, then deletes them automatically and logs what it destroyed. Insight Idea supplies the retention period. The platform enforces it without anyone remembering to.

一份永不销毁的档案，与一份过早销毁的档案同样构成风险。平台以签证获批或案件失败之日作为委托结案时点，自该日起按《行为准则》规定的期限留存材料，到期自动删除并记录销毁内容。留存期限由 Insight Idea 提供，平台负责执行——无需任何人记得去做。

Two things keep it safe. A refusal under review is held until the review concludes, so nothing is destroyed while it may still be needed. And the deletion log is permanent, because the evidence of compliance is the log rather than the absence of the file.

有两点保证其安全：处于复审程序中的拒签案件暂缓销毁，直至复审结束，避免在材料仍可能被用到时将其删除；以及销毁日志永久保留——证明合规的是这份日志，而不是文件的消失本身。
What this does not reach · 本方案覆盖不到的部分

Retention applies to documents held in the platform. Copies in shared drives and on individual laptops sit outside it, and no system-side rule reaches them. Serving documents through short-lived links rather than shared folders stops the number of copies growing, but the existing ones are a matter for Insight Idea to address as the holder of the registration. Section 14 records this as a risk with an owner.

留存规则适用于平台内保存的材料。共享云盘与个人电脑上的副本不在其管辖范围内，任何系统侧规则都无法触及。以短时效链接调阅材料、而非共享文件夹，可以阻止副本继续增加；但已经存在的副本，应由持牌方 Insight Idea 处理。第 14 节已将此列为有明确责任人的风险项。

Decision 6. Whether some matters should sit outside the platform entirely, in the firm's own system, is for Insight Idea to answer. What this document proposes is that whichever matters do sit inside it are recorded properly.

第 6 项决策。是否有部分案件应完全置于平台之外、保留在律所自有系统内，应由 Insight Idea 决定。本文件所主张的是：凡置于平台之内的案件，都应当被完整地记录下来。

8.4 Migration of existing data

8.4 既有数据迁移

The existing pipeline records import with their stages preserved. Operating stages already in use win over the Product Book's six-stage lifecycle model, because they reflect how the business actually works. Where records move between separate legal entities, the consent and disclosure basis has to exist before any record moves. It is a prerequisite, and it belongs with the lawyers rather than in the engineering backlog.

既有管道记录连同其阶段一并导入。实际在用的运营阶段优先于《产品册》的六阶段生命周期模型，因为前者反映业务的真实运作方式。凡记录需在不同法律主体之间转移的，授权同意与告知基础是前置条件，而非一项数据工作。

8.5 Getting Xiaohongshu leads into the system

8.5 让小红书的线索进入系统

Around 98.7% of leads come from Xiaohongshu and never touch the website form. Xiaohongshu does not offer the kind of API access that would let a company of this size pull those leads out automatically. That access is reserved for very large advertisers. Since the leads never arrive in the portal, none of the routing, deduplication or reporting work can do anything for them, which is why this has to be solved before anything else in Phase 1.

约 98.7% 的线索来自小红书，且从不经过官网表单。小红书并不向这一规模的公司开放能够自动拉取线索的接口——该权限只面向体量极大的广告主。由于线索根本进不了门户，自动分配、去重与报表都无从发挥作用。因此这件事必须在第一阶段的其他工作之前解决。

The website form already works. Point it at the register

官网表单已经可用，只需把它接到台账上

RedBridge Consulting has a site with an eight-step consultation form on it. It collects visa status, occupation, years of experience and English level before anyone speaks to the enquirer, which is more than a Xiaohongshu lead arrives with. It works. It is not the problem.

红桥咨询已有官网，站内的八步咨询预约表单在客户与我方接触之前，就已收集签证状态、职业、工作年限与英语水平——这比一条小红书线索所携带的信息要多得多。它本身运转正常，不是问题所在。

The problem is where the submissions go. Repointing that form so it writes to the same lead register as the Xiaohongshu import, tagged by entry path, puts website enquiries into the same triage inbox as everything else. It takes two to three days and requires no change to the site.

问题在于这些提交去了哪里。把该表单改为写入与小红书导入同一份线索台账，并按来源路径打标，官网咨询就与其余线索一同进入同一个分诊收件箱。这项改动约需两到三天，且无需改动网站本身。
Two things called booking · 两种预约并不相同

Products 1 to 11 book a product: dates, party size, payment, confirmation. Product 12 books a consultation: a calendar slot, no payment, before anything is bought. The engagement that follows is quoted, contracted and billed by milestone, so product 12 never passes through checkout. Building one flow for both is the most expensive structural mistake available here, and Section 6 keeps them apart.

产品 1 至 11 预约的是产品：日期、人数、付款、确认。产品 12 预约的是咨询：一个日程时段，不涉及付款，且发生在任何购买之前。其后的委托通过报价、签约、按里程碑收费完成，因此产品 12 全程不经过结算流程。用同一套流程承载两者，是此处代价最高的结构性错误；第 6 节将两者分开处理。

Two entry paths, not one

两条进入路径，而非一条

Website enquiries and Xiaohongshu enquiries reach the business in completely different ways, and forcing them through one process is what would break it. Website leads arrive before anyone has spoken to them, so they run the full pipeline: qualification, discovery, strategy, proposal, contract. Xiaohongshu leads are screened live, inside the chat, in the first minutes of contact. By the time any export runs, the decision has already been made.

官网咨询与小红书咨询进入公司的方式完全不同，硬性套用同一套流程正是会让它失效的原因。官网线索在任何人接触之前就已进入系统，因此走完整管道：资格判断、需求诊断、方案策略、正式提案、签约。小红书线索则是在聊天窗口内、接触后的最初几分钟里被实时筛选完毕——任何导出动作运行时，结论早已作出。

So the export serves as the ledger. Routing happens live on Xiaohongshu, before any file is downloaded. Someone downloads the report on a fixed schedule (daily is enough) and uploads it. Xiaohongshu rows land at their outcome, with the reason attached, and the sales representative adds the value on the ones that closed. From there they flow into the CRM pipeline like any other signed client.

因此，导出并非分配机制，而是台账。由专人按固定频次（每天一次即已足够）下载报表并上传。小红书的记录直接落在其最终状态上，并附带原因；已成交的由对接销售补录金额。此后它们与其他已签约客户一样，进入 CRM 管道。
Use the reasons the team already writes · 沿用团队现有的结论用语

The register already carries an outcome vocabulary staff use daily — didn't pass, can't be serviced, price, no reply, unusable contact details, closed. Those are the real reasons, so the import maps them straight through. Asking a representative to nominate which pipeline stage an Xiaohongshu lead was lost at would produce invented data, because those stages never happened.

台账中已有一套员工每天在用的结论用语——没通过、做不了、太贵了、没回复、未通过联系方式、已成交。这些才是真实原因，导入时应原样映射。若要求销售指出某条小红书线索在哪个管道阶段流失，只会得到编造的数据，因为那些阶段从未发生过。

Response time can still be measured

响应时长仍然可以度量

A daily import cannot chase a lead going cold in real time, and nothing on Xiaohongshu can. It can still measure the thing that matters. Xiaohongshu records when the person first messaged, and the portal records when the representative recorded an outcome. The gap between those two timestamps is first response, computed at import, reported the following morning. That gives an accurate median for managing the team, which is what the number is for. Live escalation while a lead is still warm is a separate capability, and it depends on the WeCom decision below, not on how often the report is uploaded.

每日导入无法实时追赶正在变冷的线索——小红书上的任何方案都做不到。但它仍能度量真正重要的指标。小红书记录了客户首次发消息的时间，门户记录了销售登记结论的时间，两个时间戳之差即为首次响应时长，在导入时计算，次日晨间呈现。这足以得到用于管理团队的准确中位数，而这正是该指标的用途。至于在线索尚有温度时自动升级提醒，属于另一项能力，取决于下文的企业微信决策，而非报表上传的频次。

It is not elegant, and it costs someone a short slot each day. But it turns a system holding one lead in eighty into a system holding all of them, and everything else in Phase 1 depends on that. Build it first, improve it later.

这个办法并不优雅，每天要占用某人一小段时间。但它能把一个八十条线索里只装一条的系统，变成一个装下全部线索的系统——而第一阶段的其余工作全都依赖于此。应当先把它做出来，之后再逐步改进。

It is worth being clear that this removes work rather than adding it. The register holds 5,076 rows with conversation transcripts and written summaries typed into them by hand. That transcription is happening today, every day. The import populates those fields automatically. The only new action anyone performs is selecting a reason on the leads they declined, and even that can be done next morning from a short exception list rather than in the moment.

有必要说清楚：这项改动是减少工作量，而不是增加。现有台账的 5,076 条记录中，聊天内容与客户摘要都是逐条手工录入的——这项转录工作今天每天都在发生。导入功能会自动填充这些字段。唯一新增的动作，是为被拒绝的线索选择一个原因；而且这一步也可以在次日晨间、从一份简短的待处理清单上批量完成，无需在接触当下操作。

Three things make the difference between this working and it becoming another abandoned process:

要让这套流程真正运转、而不是又变成一个被弃用的动作，有三点是关键：
  • One named person, one time of day. If it's nobody's specific job, it won't happen. 一个指定的人，一个固定的时间。属于所有人的任务，等于不属于任何人。
  • The upload must be forgiving. It should accept the report exactly as Xiaohongshu exports it, without anyone reformatting columns first. If the file needs preparing, the process will lapse within a fortnight. 上传功能必须足够宽容。要能直接接受小红书导出的原始报表，不需要任何人先去调整列格式。若上传前还要整理文件，这个流程两周之内就会中断。
  • Re-uploading the same file must be harmless. Reports overlap, and people will upload twice. The importer should recognise rows it has already seen and ignore them. 重复上传同一文件必须无害。报表之间会有重叠，也一定会有人传两次。导入功能应能识别已处理过的记录并自动忽略。

Conversations: keeping one source of truth

沟通记录：如何保持唯一真实来源

The same problem appears again with messaging. The portal already gives RedBridge Consulting and Insight Idea clients their own chat channel, and having every conversation attached to the right client, searchable, and still there when someone leaves, is plainly the right goal. The difficulty is that clients live in WeChat and will not move to a web chat simply because we would prefer it. A policy that fights that will lose, in the same way that running a spreadsheet alongside a CRM means the spreadsheet wins.

沟通记录上会遇到同样的问题。你已经建好了客户端聊天功能，而把所有沟通集中在一处——挂在正确的客户名下、可检索、且在员工离职后依然留存——显然是正确的目标。难点在于：客户生活在微信里，不会仅仅因为我们更希望如此就转到网页聊天。硬性规定会输给现实，正如 CRM 与表格并行时，赢的总是表格。

Two things make this workable in practice.

要让这个目标从愿望变成可实现，有两件事要做。

Pull the client back, don't push them. When a document is requested, a milestone completes or a staff member replies, the client should get a WeChat notification that opens the portal thread. The client stays in the app they already use, and the conversation of record stays in one place. Most of the traffic follows the notification.

把客户拉回来，而不是推过去。当有材料需要提交、里程碑完成、或员工回复时，客户应在微信收到通知，点击即打开门户中的对应会话。客户仍留在自己惯用的应用里，而作为正式记录的沟通则集中在一处。大部分流量会跟着通知走。

Where the conversation genuinely has to happen on WeChat, run it through WeCom. WeCom is the corporate version of WeChat. Staff use a work account, clients message it from ordinary WeChat, and, unlike personal WeChat, it has a proper interface that lets the company retain conversation records and attach them to a customer. Each external contact carries a stable identifier, and that identifier becomes the key that ties a WeChat thread to the right client record in the CRM. Capturing it at first contact is what prevents orphaned conversations later.

如果沟通确实必须发生在微信上，就走企业微信。企业微信是微信的企业版：员工使用工作账号，客户用普通微信即可与之对话；与个人微信不同，它提供正规接口，允许企业留存会话记录并关联到具体客户。每位外部联系人都有一个稳定标识，这个标识就是把某条微信会话对应到 CRM 中正确客户记录的钥匙。在首次接触时就采集它，才能避免日后出现无主的会话。
Two cautions · 两点提醒

Conversation retention has to be disclosed. WeCom shows the customer a notice, and Australian privacy obligations point the same way. This belongs in the client agreement, not in an assumption.

会话留存必须予以告知。企业微信会向客户展示提示，澳洲隐私法规的要求方向一致——这一点应写入服务协议，而不是默认客户已知悉。

Avoid any third-party tool promising to sync personal WeChat accounts automatically. They work by automating a personal account, which breaches WeChat's terms and routinely gets accounts blocked, losing the very conversation history they were bought to preserve.

应避免使用声称能自动同步个人微信账号的第三方工具。它们的原理是对个人账号做自动化操作，违反微信使用条款，账号被封是常态——结果反而丢失了当初购买它所要保全的会话记录。
Decision required · 需要决策

Name the person responsible for the daily Xiaohongshu export, and the time of day it happens. Separately, decide whether staff move to WeCom for client messaging. That one determines whether WeChat conversations can ever be part of the client record.

请指定负责每日导出小红书线索的人员及其执行时间。另需决定：员工是否改用企业微信与客户沟通——这个决定关系到微信会话今后能否成为客户档案的一部分。

11.1 Adoption is a deliverable, not an outcome

The existing portal was offered for testing and nobody tried it. That is a more useful fact than a rejection would have been, because it rules out the software as the cause. Building the software is the easier half of this problem. A system nobody uses produces the same numbers as no system.

现有门户此前已开放测试，但无人试用。这个事实比试过之后否决更有参考价值，因为它排除了软件本身的原因。3.1a 节给出了更可能的解释：门户里只装了约八十分之一的线索，因为线索都从别处进来了。这里的教训值得直说：对本项目而言，把软件做出来是较容易的那一半。一套没人用的系统，产出的漏斗数字与根本没有系统完全相同。

13.2 The return case

13.2 回报测算

The clearest return is in Phase 1, though not for the reason an earlier draft of this section gave. Routing more leads to reps is not worth much on its own, because a large share of the unrouted ones were refused deliberately and would convert at nothing like the rate of the rest.

最清晰的回报仍在第一阶段，但理由与前一版不同。单纯把更多线索分配给销售，本身价值有限——因为未被分配的线索中有相当比例是被有意拒绝的，其转化率不会与其余线索相当。

The return sits in the 2,829 leads with no disposition at all, and in the 1,553 that never moved past "arrived". Three things follow from closing that gap. Media spend gets cheaper: 93% of August leads were paid for, campaign and creative IDs are already captured on 38% of rows, and once refusals carry a reason those reasons tell the media buyer what to stop buying. Contact quality gets fixable: 206 leads went nowhere because the person never replied or the details were unusable, on a register where phone is filled 3% of the time and WeChat 44%. And the closes stop going missing. The register shows 8 against 35 advised, so every ratio the business quotes about itself is wrong by roughly four times.

回报在那 2,829 条完全没有处置结论的线索上，以及那 1,553 条从未越过已进线的线索上。补上这个缺口会带来三件事。第一，投放变便宜：8 月线索的 93% 是付费获取的，投放计划与创意 ID 已在 38% 的行上被采集；一旦拒绝都带上原因，这些原因本身就在告诉投放人员该停投什么。第二，联系方式质量变得可修复：有 206 条线索因客户未回复或联系方式不可用而中断，而该台账中电话字段仅 3% 有填写、微信仅 44%。第三，成交不再漏记：台账记录 8 笔，业务方口径 35 笔，这意味着公司对外引用的每一个比率都偏离约四倍。

Test the screening before funding anything on it · 在据此投入之前先验证筛选判断

If they convert at nothing, the screening is sound and the finding is that media should be spending less. If they convert at even a fifth of the assigned rate, the refusals have been costing more than the platform. Either answer is worth having, it takes one rep a fortnight, and it settles the argument that this section cannot settle on its own.

Two of the twelve bear on this proposal · 其中两项与本提案直接相关

Question 11 sets the milestones and stop conditions for the next twelve months. Question 12 asks what an investment committee would reject the project for today. Two of the three answers are housekeeping. The third is answered by doing what Section 11 proposes: prove demand product by product, cheaply, before building for it.

问题 12 追问：若今天将项目提交投资委员会，最可能被否决的三个理由是什么。三个理由中有两个属于内务整理，第三个由第 11 节的做法直接回应：先逐个产品、以低成本验证需求，再为其建设。
